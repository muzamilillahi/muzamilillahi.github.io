1. MANCHAR LAKE

Manchar Lake is South Asia's largest natural freshwater lake, located in Dadu and Jamshoro districts, Sindh, Pakistan. It lies west of the Indus River along the Kirthar Mountains, spanning 150–200 km² in dry periods and expanding over 500–800 km² during monsoon flood peaks.

MANCHAR LAKE BASIN OVERVIEW

2. CATCHMENT HYDROLOGY & INFLOWS

The lake receives inflow from three primary sources: flash torrent floods from the Kirthar Mountains, agricultural effluent via the Main Nara Valley Drain, and bi-directional river flow from the Indus River through the Aral and Danister canals.

3. MOHANA COMMUNITY HERITAGE

Indigenous Mohana Houseboats on Manchar Lake

The Mohana (Mirbahar) fishing community has inhabited wooden houseboats on Manchar Lake for centuries. Ecological stress and rising water salinity have drastically reduced fish yields, threatening their traditional livelihood.

4. WATER QUALITY & ECOLOGICAL IMPACT

Untreated industrial and agricultural drainage has degraded water quality, pushing Total Dissolved Solids past 4,000–5,000 mg/L and impacting local public health and aquatic ecosystems.

5. 2022 SINDH FLOOD CRISIS

2022 Monsoonal Flood Relief Cuts

During the August 2022 super-floods, record rainfall drove lake levels to 122.5 ft RL. Controlled breaches were made at LS Ring Bund to protect downstream cities like Sehwan Sharif.
